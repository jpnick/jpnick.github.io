Travel Blog

Exploring the world provides new perspectives. Here are some highlights from my recent trips.

Kyoto, Japan

Autumn in the Historic Capital

Visited: November 2025

Visiting the Fushimi Inari Shrine early in the morning before the crowds arrived was a surreal experience. The hike up the mountain offered beautiful views. Trying authentic matcha and local street food at the Nishiki Market was definitely a culinary highlight.

"There is a palpable sense of peace walking through paths that have been tread upon for centuries."
Central Europe

From Budapest to the Matterhorn

Visited: August 2025

A memorable journey stretching from the historic thermal baths and stunning architecture of Budapest, through the heart of Europe, culminating in the breathtaking alpine vistas beneath the iconic Matterhorn in Zermatt.

Hawaii, USA

Aloha Spirit and Volcanic Landscapes

Visited: January 2024

Our journey to Hawaii was a breathtaking exploration of contrasts. From the moment we landed, the warm embrace of the "Aloha spirit" was palpable—a genuine hospitality deeply rooted in native culture. We spent our days navigating the diverse microclimates of the islands, venturing from lush, cascading waterfalls to the stark, lunar-like landscapes of Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park.

Standing near the smoking caldera of Kīlauea and feeling the raw, geological power of the earth was a humbling experience that puts human existence into perspective.

"The Aloha spirit isn't just a greeting; it's a way of living in harmony with people and the powerful land that shapes them."
Beijing, China

Ancient History in a Modern Metropolis

Visited: November 2024

Beijing offers a dizzying blend of ancient history and rapid modernization. Standing at the center of the city, the Forbidden City proved to be an architectural marvel of staggering proportions. Wandering through its endless courtyards and intricate wooden pavilions felt like stepping back into the Ming and Qing dynasties.

Equally unforgettable was the hike along the Mutianyu section of the Great Wall. Tracing the stone pathways as they snaked across the jagged ridges of the mountains in the crisp autumn air was a bucket-list moment that photos simply cannot do justice.

"To climb the Great Wall is to feel the immense weight of centuries of history beneath your feet."
